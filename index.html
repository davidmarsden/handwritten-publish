Four ways to get human-made things onto the web with less machinery in the way. Write by hand, build a richer post, upload a mixed batch, or move prepared Markdown into a private working draft before publishing it unchanged.

01

Writing Hand

Write on reMarkable, use its transcription when you want it, and send the result straight to Micro.blog.
