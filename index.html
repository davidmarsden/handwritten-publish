Small tools for human publishing

Helping
Hand

Three ways to get human-made things onto the web with less machinery in the way. Write by hand, build a post from scans and photos, or just throw a batch of files at Micro.blog.

01

Writing Hand

Write on reMarkable, use its transcription when you want it, and send the result straight to Micro.blog.

Live · setup first Open Writing Hand → From paper to web at the push of a pen.
02

Publish Hand

Build a web-ready post from handwriting, scans, PDFs and photos. Arrange pages, add links and metadata, then publish to Micro.blog.

Live Open Publish Hand →
03

BUM Hand

Batch Uploader for Micro.blog. Drop in a bunch of images, upload them in one go, and copy the resulting URLs, Markdown or HTML.

Live Open BUM Hand →
